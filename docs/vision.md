# StudentOS Vision

## What is StudentOS?

StudentOS is a long-term project focused on creating a persistent academic workspace for students.

The goal is to ensure that a student's academic environment, including files, projects, assignments, and learning resources, remains accessible across different devices and computers.

## Problem Statement

Engineering students frequently use shared computer laboratories where they cannot reliably access their previous work on different systems.

Academic data is often scattered across devices, storage platforms, and local machines, creating continuity, accessibility, and privacy challenges.

## Vision

StudentOS aims to become an intelligent academic workspace that preserves, organizes, and eventually understands a student's digital academic life.

Students should be able to log in from any device and continue their academic work without worrying about where their files, projects, or assignments are stored.

## Long-Term Direction

In future versions, StudentOS may evolve to include:

- AI-powered academic assistance
- Intelligent task and study planning
- Assignment and project management
- Institution integration
- Learning analytics
- Personalized academic recommendations

## Current Focus

The focus of StudentOS v0.1 is not to build a complete operating system.

The focus is to build the foundation of a Persistent Academic Workspace.
